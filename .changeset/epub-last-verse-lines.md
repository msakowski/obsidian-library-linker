Fix offline quotes of the last verse of a chapter stopping after its first line when the verse runs on in the next paragraphs, as in poems (Psalm 83:18, Habakkuk 3:19). Acrostic letters and subscriptions such as "ב [Beth]" are no longer appended to the verse before them, matching the online text. An offline Bible imported before this fix keeps these errors until it is imported again.
