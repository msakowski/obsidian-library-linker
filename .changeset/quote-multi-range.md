Fix Bible quotes for multi-chapter references (`Matt 3:1-4:11`): the reference is now looked up chapter by chapter, since a citation provider resolves a single verse range inside a single chapter, and the parts are stitched into one quote labelled with the whole reference. A part that cannot be fetched fails the whole quote rather than writing a partial one. Offline, a part that runs to the end of a chapter stops at its last verse, verses the NWT omits (such as Matt. 17:21) are skipped, and any other missing verse or a range past the end of a chapter fails so the online fallback can take over. This applies to the quote commands and the context menu. Online quotes no longer include the chapter number of a chapter's first verse, which a multi-chapter quote showed in the middle of the text (`… approved.” 4 Then Jesus …`). A quote spans at most 3 chapters: for a longer reference (`Ps 1:1-150:6`) a notice is shown and nothing is fetched or inserted. Reconverting an existing cross-chapter link keeps its end chapter (`40003001-40004011` no longer becomes `40003001-40003011`). With online fallback turned off, a failed quote now says why (the offline Bible is not imported, or lacks the verses) instead of asking to try again, and nothing is requested online.
